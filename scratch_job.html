Director/Deputy Director – Cultural Affairs

Parul University Goa is a not-for-profit, world-class institution, nestled in the heart of Goa, established in 2025 by the Parul Education Trust to redefine higher education through an immersive experience of quality and excellence at the shores of India’s coastal paradise.

Key Responsibilities:

  1. Strategic Leadership & Alignment
    Lead Cultural Affairs in alignment with institutional vision, integrating cultural initiatives with academic, student development, and institutional objectives.
  2. Cultural Planning & Flagship Events
    Plan and oversee the annual cultural roadmap and flagship events, ensuring quality, inclusivity, and institutional relevance.
  3. Execution & Impact Monitoring
    Translate strategy into action, monitor implementation, and assess outcomes through participation, impact, and feedback metrics.
  4. Student Engagement & Talent Development
    Promote student participation, nurture cultural talent, and facilitate inter-institutional and competitive cultural exposure.
  5. Governance, Budgeting & Compliance
    Manage budgets and ensure transparent, compliant governance of all cultural activities.
  6. Partnerships & Cultural Branding
    Build external partnerships and enhance institutional cultural visibility and reputation.
  7. Team Leadership & Performance
    Lead and evaluate the cultural affairs team to ensure effective delivery and continuous capacity building.
  8. Documentation & Continuous Improvement
    Maintain systematic documentation and reporting to support audits and drive continuous improvement.

Qualifications & Eligibility:

  • Postgraduate degree in Arts, Humanities, Cultural Studies, Event Management, or related field. PhD is preferred.
  • Strong strategic, operational, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Proven experience in large-scale event and budget management.
    Minimum experience - Director - 10+ years & Deputy Director - 5+ years

Note: Qualifications & Experience as per AICTE / UGC or other relevant governing body norms.
